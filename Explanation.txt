Project Goals Revisited:

To explore how remote work correlates with mental health conditions.
To identify patterns in stress, productivity, and work-life balance.
To determine which factors contribute to improved mental health in remote work environments.
To provide data-driven recommendations for employers and policymakers aimed at enhancing employee mental well-being.
Create web app that will help employers analyse and predict mental health condition(stress) of their workers.The system will give a recommendations to the employer towards the employee condition.
